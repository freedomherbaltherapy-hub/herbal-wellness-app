# 🌿 Herbal Wellness App

Welcome to the Herbal Wellness App — a digital platform designed to provide accessible educational information about herbs, traditional herbal practices, and general wellness.

## 🌱 About the App

The Herbal Wellness App helps users explore information about medicinal plants and natural wellness practices in an easy-to-use digital environment.

The platform is being developed by **Freedom Herbal Therapy**.

## ✨ Planned Features

- 🌿 Herbal plant information
- 🔎 Search herbs by name
- 📚 Herbal education and wellness articles
- 💡 Natural wellness tips
- ⚠️ Herbal safety information
- 🧪 Information about traditional herbal preparations
- 📱 Mobile-friendly interface
- 📞 Contact and consultation information
- 🏠 Freedom Herbal Therapy profile

## 🎯 Project Goals

The goal of this project is to make reliable herbal and wellness education easier to access while encouraging users to make informed health decisions.

## 🏥 Health & Safety Disclaimer

The information provided by this application is for educational and general wellness purposes only.

It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. Herbal products may interact with medicines or may not be appropriate for everyone.

Users with medical conditions, pregnant or breastfeeding individuals, children, or people taking medication should consult a qualified healthcare professional before using herbal products.
